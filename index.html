The Billion-Dollar Bazaar

Researcher · Writer · Designer
Beginner’s guideThe Billion-Dollar BazaarD. Batra
A publishing house has formally approached me about developing it into a full-length book.

Independently researched, wrote and designed a 45-page beginner-friendly guide to the share market.

The goal was to make investing less intimidating through familiar examples, personal case studies, humour and a conversational voice, without assuming readers already knew the jargon.

The approach

It began with one question: why should the stock market feel like a foreign language? The guide starts from zero and explains share-market basics and stock-related ideas step by step. Structure, tone, examples and page design were all done independently.

Research & reader feedback

The research drew on Stock to Riches, The Intelligent Investor and Common Sense on Mutual Funds, alongside my own online research.

Readers gave both encouraging and critical feedback, which showed me what was landing and where an explanation needed to be clearer.

What’s next

I’m developing it into a full-length book.
